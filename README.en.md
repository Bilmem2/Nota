> You can also enter the app without an API key — a key is required for AI features.

## What Can It Do?

- **Lesson** — Explains your material at university level with conceptual depth
- **Study Guide** — Key concepts, common traps, and likely exam questions
- **Visual Summary** — Renders the topic as tables, timelines, or comparison cards
- **Concept Map** — Displays concepts and their relationships in an interactive map
- **Quiz Mode** — Generates multiple choice, short answer, and essay questions; evaluates instantly
- **Mixed Exam** — Combines multiple topics for a midterm/final-style mock exam
- **AI Chat** — Ask anything about the material, analyze your weak points
- **Archive** — Auto-saves your studies so you can pick up right where you left off
